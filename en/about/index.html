About 喝酒去

Four content repositories and four site repositories, with one identity and evidence system.

Hejiu helps readers understand drinks, learn how to enjoy them, read a little culture, and find real places to visit. The four sites answer “what is this?”, “how do I enjoy it?”, “what stories sit beyond the glass?”, and “where can I go?”.

This is the curated edition of 29 October 2026: 166 pages and 83 entities across four sites, all source-verified and bilingual. The 553 legacy archive records that could not stand alone, including all 57 poem excerpts, have been retired from publication; their old URLs now return 404, and their source text remains only in the internal migration ledger and backup.

How content is organised

  • Four content repositories: alcoholic-beverages, alcohol-guide, alcohol-culture and alcohol-atlas.
  • Four site repositories: site-alcoholic-beverages, site-alcohol-guide, site-alcohol-culture and site-alcohol-atlas.
  • Every object has a language-independent stable ID. Chinese and English are maintained separately, and each English file records the hash of the Chinese source it translated.
  • Relationships, sources, rights and translation status are checked by a real validator rather than guessed at build time.

What this edition covers

This site currently publishes 54 pages with no pending review previews. The edition covers 15 drink styles, 31 drinks, one brand, 16 historic bars and cafés, plus guides, recipes, glassware, culture articles and a visitor place; all 9 prototype samples have been migrated. Except for one unpublished legacy-taxonomy draft, every record passes source, rights and translation checks.

Sources and limits

Every published page has at least one source independent of the old site; legacy text is no longer copied into public pages. Code is MIT and content is CC BY 4.0; third-party standards, trademarks, photographs and quoted texts are not covered by that licence.

Claims without checked sources are not presented as reviewed, and we do not invent brands, opening information, events or translations to fill pages. Current opening hours, ticket prices and booking rules should be checked with the venue's official channels.

This is a static site. It does not provide accounts, purchases, bookings or cross-device sync.
